### 🛠 Step 3: Productivity Techniques to Use
1.  **The Pomodoro Technique:** Study for 25 minutes, break for 5 minutes. After 4 cycles, take a longer 20-minute break.
2.  **Time Blocking:** Assign specific subjects to specific times (e.g., "Math is only for Mornings").
3.  **The 2-Minute Rule:** If a study task takes less than 2 minutes (e.g., emailing a professor, organizing files), do it immediately.
4.  **Environment Design:** Keep your study space clutter-free and phone-free during deep work blocks.
